VISIT THE GREAT SALT LAKE!

The Great Salt Lake is one of the most important bird and wildlife refuges in the American interior west. Come and visit to see thousands of birds and dozens of species!

Birds by Numbers

  • Ducks- the main species of duck seen in the Great Salt Lake are Cinnamon Teals, Green-winged Teals and Northern Shovelers. Numbers fluctuate throughout the year but these can often be seen in the hundreds. Other common ducks include Gadwall, American Wigeon, Canvasback and Redhead, as well as Wood Ducks which are not common but are basically only found here.
